## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of our software seriously. If you find a security vulnerability, please help us fix it.

### How to Report

1.  **Do not open a public issue.** Publicly reporting a vulnerability can put the entire community at risk.
2.  Please send an email to [odysseaskalaitsides@gmail.com](mailto:odysseaskalaitsides@gmail.com) with the subject line "Security Vulnerability: [Guest Relations AI App]".
3.  Include as much detail as possible:
    *   Steps to reproduce the vulnerability.
    *   The version of the software you are running.
    *   Any relevant configuration details.
    *   Potential impact of the vulnerability.

### What Happens Next

1.  We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 48 hours.
2.  We will investigate the issue and determine its severity.
3.  We will work on a fix and test it thoroughly.
4.  We will release a patch and notify the community.
5.  We will credit you for your discovery (unless you prefer to remain anonymous).

### Responsible Disclosure

We ask that you give us a reasonable amount of time to fix the issue before disclosing it to the public. We aim to resolve critical issues within 1 week and other issues within 2-4 weeks.

Thank you for helping keep our community safe!
